9. AI-Assisted Document Generation

Important disclosure: 3CH0 uses artificial intelligence to assist in generating compliance documents. All users should be aware of the following.

  • AI Involvement: Documents produced by 3CH0 are generated with the assistance of AI language models. The information you provide is submitted to our AI processing infrastructure to produce your output.
  • No Automated Legal Decisions: We do not make legally binding decisions about you solely through automated processing. AI outputs are tools to assist your compliance — not final legal determinations.
  • Review Recommended: AI-generated content may contain inaccuracies, omissions, or inapplicable provisions. You are responsible for reviewing all output before relying on it. We strongly recommend that a qualified legal or compliance professional reviews any generated documents.
  • Data Submitted to AI: Information you provide during document generation is processed by Anthropic (Claude AI) on our behalf. Anthropic's usage policies prohibit using API inputs to train their models. Nevertheless, you should avoid submitting sensitive personal data beyond what is strictly necessary for your compliance document.
  • Right to Human Review: If you have concerns about any automated output that affects you, you may contact us to request a human review.

10. Your Rights

You have meaningful rights over your personal information. These rights apply under both POPIA (for South African residents) and GDPR (where applicable).

Rights Under POPIA and GDPR

  • Right of Access: Request a copy of all personal information we hold about you
  • Right to Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Right to Deletion: Request erasure of your personal information, subject to any overriding legal obligations
  • Right to Portability: Receive your personal information in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format
  • Right to Object: Object to processing based on legitimate interests or direct marketing purposes
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Where processing is based on consent, withdraw it at any time without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing
  • Right to Restrict Processing: Request that we limit how we use your data in certain circumstances
  • Right to Document Access: Download your generated compliance documents at any time while your account is active

How to Exercise Your Rights

Submit your request to 3CH0.The.Only@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days. We may need to verify your identity before processing your request. Requests are free of charge unless manifestly unfounded or excessive.

Right to Lodge a Complaint

If you believe we have not handled your personal information in accordance with applicable law, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the relevant supervisory authority:

  • South Africa (POPIA): The Information Regulator — www.inforegulator.org.za, complaints email: complaints.IR@justice.gov.za
  • EU/EEA (GDPR): Your local Data Protection Authority

We ask that you contact us in the first instance so we have the opportunity to resolve your concern directly.

12. Data Breach Notification

In the event of a personal information security compromise (data breach), we will take the following steps:

  • Internal Response: We will promptly investigate and contain the breach upon discovery
  • Regulator Notification: Where required by law (including POPIA and GDPR), we will notify the relevant supervisory authority within the applicable timeframe (72 hours under GDPR; as soon as reasonably possible under POPIA)
  • Individual Notification: Where a breach is likely to result in a high risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify affected individuals without undue delay using the contact information we hold for you
  • Remediation: We will take all reasonable steps to mitigate the impact of the breach and prevent recurrence

13. Security Measures

We implement reasonable and appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. These measures include:

  • Data Encryption: Encryption of data in transit using TLS and, where applicable, at rest
  • Access Controls: Role-based internal access limitations to personal information
  • Third-Party Security: We select third-party service providers that implement appropriate security standards
  • Ongoing Review: Periodic review of our security practices as our platform evolves

No system can guarantee absolute security. If you become aware of any potential security vulnerability, please notify us immediately at 3CH0.The.Only@gmail.com.
